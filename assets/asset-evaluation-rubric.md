## Evaluation Rubric

### Criteria

| Criterion | Weight | 5 (Excellent) | 3 (Adequate) | 1 (Poor) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Accuracy | 30% | Fully correct, well-sourced | Mostly correct, minor gaps | Significant errors |
| Completeness | 25% | All requirements addressed | Most requirements met | Key areas missing |
| Clarity | 20% | Clear, well-structured | Understandable but unpolished | Confusing or disorganised |
| Relevance | 15% | Directly addresses the brief | Generally on-topic | Off-topic or tangential |
| Originality | 10% | Fresh insights, unique angle | Competent but predictable | Generic or derivative |

### Scoring
- **20-25:** Excellent — ready to use
- **15-19:** Good — minor revisions needed
- **10-14:** Adequate — significant revision needed
- **Below 10:** Poor — rework required
